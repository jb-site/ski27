Flaine

· about 1½ hr from Geneva

Where we went last year. Car-free resort centre with the ski school and lifts close by.

April: very good. The north-facing main bowl holds snow well, but lower runs can become slushy and links towards the lower villages are more vulnerable to warmth.

Kids: greens and gentle blues offer easy progression.

Les Arcs

· about 1 hr 50 from Chambéry

Part of Paradiski, a very large ski area, with 425 km of pistes.

April: excellent. The high, sheltered, largely north-facing bowl preserves snow particularly well into April, and about 70% of the pistes are above 2,000 m. The season ends 20 April 2027, so 3–10 April is fine.

Kids: ski school from age 3. Wide, gentle blues around Arc 2000 suit the kids well, and you can reach La Plagne on blues.

Obergurgl, Austria

· about 1½ hr from Innsbruck

High up, snow-sure, and mostly easy runs. Suits families with younger kids. 112 km of piste, 25 lifts.

April: best chance of good snow. The village is at 1,930 m and the lifts go to about 3,000 m. Snow usually holds into April, when lower resorts turn to slush. The season runs to at least 11 April, so 3–10 April is fine.

Kids: over half the runs are easy, and Hochgurgl is almost all blue. Two kids-only practice zones, away from the main pistes. Ski school from age 3, in groups of 5–6. Strong skiers may run out of runs.

Courchevel 1850

· about 1½ hr from Chambéry

The top village of Courchevel, in Les 3 Vallées (about 600 km of pistes). Snow-sure and central, but expensive, and some pistes get crowded in high season.

April: good snow prospects. 85% of Courchevel's slopes are above 1,800 m, and the season runs to 18 April 2027, so 3–10 April is fine. Easy runs near resort can become heavy/slushy after lunch in warm weather.

Kids: excellent easy terrain, with broad greens and mellow blues. ESF's Village des Enfants has beginner zones with covered magic carpets, and kids at 2nd star and above meet at the Croisette snow front. Group lessons run 9:30–11:45 and 14:15–16:45. A course starts on 3 April.
