April snow and kids at a glance

Likely conditions for 3–10 April, in order of snow confidence.

  • Obergurgl: best chance of good snow. High slopes and extensive snowmaking make it very reliable at Easter. Plenty of gentle blues, especially in Hochgurgl; lower runs can still soften on warm afternoons.
  • Arc 1950: excellent. Wide, gentle blues around Arc 2000 suit the kids well. The high, sheltered, largely north-facing bowl preserves snow particularly well into April.
  • Flaine: very good. Greens and gentle blues offer easy progression. The north-facing main bowl holds snow well, but lower runs can become slushy and links towards the lower villages are more vulnerable to warmth.
  • Courchevel 1850: good snow prospects, excellent easy terrain. Broad greens and mellow blues are ideal for building confidence. Northerly slopes and excellent grooming help, but easy runs near resort can become heavy/slushy after lunch in warm weather.
