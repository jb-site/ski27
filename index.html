Kids

  • Over half the runs are easy. Hochgurgl is almost all blue.
  • Two kids-only practice zones, away from the main pistes.
  • Ski school from age 3, in groups of 5–6.
  • Strong skiers may run out of runs.

April

  • The village is at 1,930 m and the lifts go to about 3,000 m.
  • Snow usually holds into April, when lower resorts turn to slush.
  • The season runs to at least 11 April, so 3–10 April is fine.
  • 112 km of piste, 25 lifts.
